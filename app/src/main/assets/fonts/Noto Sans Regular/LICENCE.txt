The letter shapes the map writes track names with.

They are cut from Noto Sans, which may be used, changed and passed on freely under the SIL Open Font
Licence, Version 1.1. The copyright line that licence asks to be carried with them is:

    Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic)

What is in this folder is not the whole font but the three drawing ranges the map asks for: the
everyday letters, the accented ones, and the marks and symbols beside them. A map draws its letters
from files like these rather than from a font installed on the phone, which is why they travel with
the app and are handed out by the app's own little server.

The licence itself is at https://scripts.sil.org/OFL
